LAST QUARTER

Two-player versus fighting · build 0.2

HOW TO PLAY

5 drops a coin. 1 starts a one-player run against the CPU. 2 starts player versus player. Player 2 can also press 2 mid-fight to challenge.

Each player has an 8-way stick and six buttons: light, medium and heavy punch on the top row, light, medium and heavy kick on the bottom row. The keys are printed on the panel above.

Hold back to block. Block low kicks crouching and jump attacks standing. Light and medium attacks cancel into special moves when they connect.

Arcade sticks and gamepads work: the first one plugged in is Player 1, the second is Player 2. No numpad? Switch KEYS to the shared-board layout. M mutes.

MOVE LIST

D = down, F = toward the other fighter, B = away, DF and DB are the diagonals. Roll the stick through the directions, then press any punch (P) or any kick (K). A heavier button gives a stronger version. Gold moves spend a full super bar.
